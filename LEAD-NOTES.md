## 🔴 Location-page question list — ask these per area

**One pass per area, roughly twenty minutes each.** This is the content job behind the twenty pages, and the honest position is: **eight genuinely distinct pages beat twenty thin ones.** Twenty find-and-replace pages are the doorway pattern and get suppressed sitewide.

Per area — Acworth, Alpharetta, Atlanta, Austell, Canton, Cartersville, Dallas (GA), Douglasville, Emerson, Hiram, Holly Springs, Kennesaw, Lithia Springs, Mableton, Marietta, Powder Springs, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Smyrna, Villa Rica:

1. **What do you actually run into there?** Gated communities, HOA rules on driveway work, condo decks, apartment car parks, downtown parking garages, office parks, no shade.
2. **Is there usually a tap and an outlet available?** (Ties to the water/power question.)
3. **Which services does that area actually buy** — and does the mix genuinely differ from the next town over? ⚠️ Honest answers only; if it's the same, the page says something else instead.
4. **Is it inside the core radius, or does it carry a travel charge or a minimum job value?**
5. **One real before/after shot from that area.** (This is what makes a location page unarguably genuine.)
6. **Anything landmark-level only somebody who works there would know** — where you park, what the roads are like, which weeks are busy.
7. **Any area you'd rather drop**, or any you cover that isn't on the Google list?
